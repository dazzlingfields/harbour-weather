Harbour's interface has been rebuilt around wind, sea state and your time on the water.

- A field-book visual style: warm paper, dark ink, thin rules, restrained condition colours and a new bow-and-water logo. Reworked vector icons also appear in the app launcher and splash screen.
- Wind leads the overview, with a direction instrument, waves and swell, compact weather readings, a 24/48-hour wind trend and optional detailed hourly figures.
- Hourly wind arrows point toward the labelled source direction, consistent with the overview direction instrument.
- The mobile Forecast tab fits all seven days across the viewport. Hourly forecasts run vertically in a bounded scroll area; tap an hour for the complete forecast. The page no longer stretches sideways.
- Plan a trip from the shortcut beside the model selector or the overview form. Choose a leaving time and duration to see a trip summary, model gust/wave highs, confidence, daylight flags and the hourly conditions for that exact window. Trip rows scroll within the popup. Times use NZST/NZDT and partial hours are included.
- Separate settings tabs for Units and Trip limits. Choose knots/km/h/m/s/mph, metres/feet, Celsius/Fahrenheit, km/nautical miles/miles, hPa/inHg and mm/inches. Units apply across the overview, forecasts, map measurements/scale, model ranges and tides. Changes preserve the underlying physical limits.
- Additional filters for maximum swell, minimum wave period, rain chance, visibility and Blend confidence, plus minimum window duration, planning horizon and daylight-only matching. Optional missing data never passes a selected filter.
- Smaller dialogs, a fixed Save action, clean day tiles, nested hourly detail popups, compact map controls and reduced-motion support.

Harbour Blend remains the default. Its confidence index reflects model agreement and coverage, not a calibrated probability of forecast accuracy. Personal trip filters are not boat safety ratings.

Local validation: 42 automated tests passed, including unit round trips, expanded filters, direction arrows, NZ time/DST, partial trip hours and missing coverage. Shared assets synchronized to Android. Physical Android testing and a new APK build remain pending publication.
